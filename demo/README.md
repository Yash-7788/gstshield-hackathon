# Fictional manual-import files

These files describe a fictional pump invoice for ₹1,00,000 goods plus ₹18,000 tax in October 2026. They are not government records. Use the demonstration registration 27ABCDE1234F1Z5 and month 2026-10.

1. Open More tools, then Sources.
2. Choose Purchase and the five-column purchase format. Upload simple-purchase.csv. Read and acknowledge its assumptions, then confirm the parsed row. Its total tax is known; its individual tax components remain unknown. A matching GST record therefore still requires component review.
3. Choose GST statement and the GSTR-2B JSON format. Upload gst-statement-b2b.json and confirm its row. This supported B2B example records the actual component split; it is a fictional file, not automatic government fetching.
4. Open Reconciliation, select these two confirmed sources and review the result. To demonstrate a fully known invoice, use the existing sample invoice PDF in output/pdf or the complete component purchase CSV already provided in this folder. AI reading needs configured Gemini and explicit upload consent.

No sample proves delivery, government IRN authenticity, legal credit eligibility or an actual bank transfer. Add the order, receipt and reviewed payment facts in Invoice desk for a four-way review.
